# Research notes

Reviewed October 18, 2026. These sources inform editorial judgment; they do not prove that more graphics improve retention for every podcast or channel. No source text, template implementation, font or image is bundled here.

| Source | Supported principle | Application and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Adobe: Create lower-third graphics](https://helpx.adobe.com/sa_ar/after-effects/how-to/lower-third-animations.html) | Brief, easily read information, simple backing and purposeful entry/exit motion | Short verified name/role; adapt hold to actual copy rather than animate constantly |
| [Brame: Effective Educational Videos](https://doi.org/10.1187/cbe.16-03-0125) | Signaling, segmenting and removal of extraneous material help manage cognitive load | Select a few spoken key phrases, direct attention to the exact screenshot region and build diagrams in sequence. Educational-video evidence is not a guaranteed social-media engagement result. |
| [W3C: Contrast Minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html) | Text/background contrast thresholds of 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text | Use controlled text plates; inspect downscaled final video. The guidance originates in web accessibility and is not a video compliance certificate. |
| [W3C: Three Flashes or Below Threshold](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/three-flashes-or-below-threshold.html) | Flash rate, luminance and area affect photosensitive risk | Prefer nonflashing highlights; no need to approach the threshold for informative overlays |
| [Adobe: Safe margins](https://helpx.adobe.com/ca/after-effects/desktop/get-started/preferences-and-settings/preferences.html) | Action/title guides help protect important information across display/crop contexts | Set delivery-specific guides and reserve caption/platform UI space; a generic inset cannot guarantee every destination's safe area |
| [HyperFrames: Rendering](https://hyperframes.app/docs/3-guides/4-rendering) | Local seek-driven rendering and MOV/ProRes 4444 alpha overlays | Render cue-length graphics and composite independently from the episode audio. Confirm flags with the pinned CLI; online examples and installed versions can differ. |
| [Official HyperFrames repository](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) | Maintained authoring contracts, CLI and registry | Resolve current installed skills, catalog and exact project version before writing code. Reuse suitable lower-third/highlight primitives; do not vendor a second copy of their workflow. |

The restrained motion durations, reading-time estimate and 5–10% horizontal inset in [design.md](design.md) are editable craft defaults derived from practical composition needs. They are not universal standards or experimentally established thresholds. Human review on the actual footage and destination remains necessary.
